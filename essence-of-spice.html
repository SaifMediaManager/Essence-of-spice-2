Our Story

Slow flame, open garden,
quietly good food.

Essence of Spice sits back from the University Bypass in a landscaped, pergola-covered garden — long wooden-roofed dining bays strung with warm lights, a fountain at the edge of the seating, and lawns that run out into the dark. Everything on the menu is prepared pure vegetarian, from tandoor-fresh breads to slow-cooked dals.

Guests come back for the calm of the space as much as the food — it's become a regular pick for family dinners, quiet evenings and small celebrations, with a kitchen team that pays close attention to how each dish is spiced and plated.
